GameSpace Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how the GameSpace Android app ("the app"), published by ASH TECH, handles information.

Information we collect

We don't collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts and no sign-up. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software, and it doesn't need an internet connection.

Information stored on your device

To save your progress, the app keeps game data in a local database on your phone: high scores, unlocked levels, game settings, saved boards and drawings, favorites, and play-time statistics. This data never leaves your device and we can't see it. You can delete it at any time in the app (Profile → Reset all progress, or Reset progress on a game's page), or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app doesn't ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because it collects no personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how information is handled (for example, by adding advertising), we will update this page and the effective date before that version is released.
